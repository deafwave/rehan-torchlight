The Bundles

Purchases that only pay off together. Each card is one engine: buy its pieces in any mix, but don't stop at half a card — a Fervor glove without the boots that hold Fervor is a dead slot. Cards are numbered in the order the engines come online; a needs line is the only hard rule.

The Linear Upgrades

The same slot, bought again and again: i82 → i86 → priceless → mirror-worthy. Almost none of these rungs change how the build works — they are the same item with bigger numbers, which is why they can be bought in any order, on any budget, without a rework.

Every rung is priced against one fixed reference build: every other slot pinned at the top of its own ladder, only this slot's item swapped, model re-run. Only the relative gain is shown — absolute DPS shifts with every surrounding detail, but each step's % is what that upgrade buys you. Read a ladder downward as “what I give up by stopping here,” not as a route you must walk in order. Rungs marked ⚠ are the exception: they change the rules rather than the numbers, and they belong to a bundle above.

Rare rungs also unfold stat by stat: each crafted line's marginal ΔDPS (model re-run with that one line removed) and its ember pool — basic ×1, advanced ×3, ultimate ×6 craft cost — sorted by ΔDPS per cost, so the right column is the order to craft or shop the lines in.
