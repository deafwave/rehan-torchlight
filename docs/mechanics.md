# Spectral Slash (Rehan, SS12/SS13) — Modifier Classification

Sources: tlidb.com wiki, official game manual mirror (legacy.torchcodex.com), XD dev FAQs (taptap.io),
local tlidb caches (`.claude/skills/tli-build/cache/SS12.5-*.json`), the build export
`data/Rehan.json` (loadout 0 "Full precise auras"). Cache citations quote in-game wording.

## Pipeline summary (9 stages) — and where research contradicts the plan

1. **Weapon base** — mainhand implicit phys (178–178) scaled by *local* `+% Gear Physical Damage`, plus local flat adds ("Adds X–X ... to the gear").
2. **Global flat added** — "Adds X–X Physical Damage to Attacks and Spells" (rings, Vortex Heart), scaled by the skill's added-damage effectiveness.
3. **Skill weapon %** — "Deals 127% Weapon Attack Damage" (finisher) / 406% (starter 1).
4. **Conversion 100% phys→cold** (Prophet tree node) — happens after base assembly; the hit is cold, but keeps its physical origin tags.
5. **Increased pool** — ALL non-"additional" `+% damage` modifiers (physical, cold, elemental, attack, melee, area, global, strength? see below) sum into one `(1 + Σ)` factor. Confirmed: `Damage = Base × (1 + all non-additional %) × (1 + additional₁) × (1 + additional₂)…` (tlidb Damage_Calculation).
6. <a id="additional"></a>**Additional Damage Bonus — a product, not a pool** — every "+X% additional damage" source is its **own `(1 + X)` multiplier**; the in-game "Additional Damage Bonus" character-sheet stat is `∏(1 + Xᵢ) − 1`, NOT a summed pool. **Confirmed by user toggle 2026-07-17**: removing a single +12% additional line moved the live sheet **2165% → 1922%** — i.e. `÷1.12` (`22.65× / 1.12 = 20.22×`), which a sum cannot produce (it would read 2153%). Matches the tlidb formula on line 5. Encoded in two places that must agree: `buildParser` compounds the lines **within** each `additional.*` bucket, and `damageModel`/`catalog.applyStat` multiply the **buckets** — together `∏(1 + Xᵢ)`. `Damage = Base × (1 + Σ increased) × ∏(1 + additionalᵢ) × (1 + enemy-takenᵢ)…`. Willpower, Pure Heart and skill/gem levels are simply additional multipliers like the rest (a level scales the skill's *base*, ×1.10/×1.08 per level, [#skill-levels](#skill-levels)). *(Reverses the 2026-07-17 "summed pool" note, which misread the single sheet figure as additive — it is the product collapsed to one display value. See [#assumptions](#assumptions) on the resulting absolute-DPS shift.)*
7. **Enemy-taken layers** — Frostbite rating, Paralysis, Timid curse, Cold Infiltration: each a separate multiplier on the defender side.
8. **Crit** — chance = Final Crit Rating / 100 (no enemy-level curve); expected multiplier `1 + chance×(critdmg−1)`, base crit damage 150%.
9. **Mitigation** — computed **after** conversion, in **two independent layers** (user-corrected 2026-07-15, supersedes the earlier "cold res only" reading): monsters default to ~30% **elemental resistance** AND ~30% **elemental reduction from armor**. `mult = (1 − (res − elemental pen)) × (1 − (armor reduction − armor mitigation pen))`, and **both penetrations can push their layer negative** (dev-confirmed for res; user-confirmed for armor).

**Contradictions with the plan found by research:**
- **Task 3 clamp is wrong**: `test_penetration_cannot_push_res_below_zero` contradicts the dev FAQ ("Penetrate 75% res by 100% → treated as −25%"). Remove the `max(0, …)` clamp.
- **Combo Finisher Amplification is not `(1 + amp)`**: actual formula is `Finisher Hit Damage × (1 + ConsumedComboPoints × amp)`. *(Partially superseded 2026-07-16: only "Combo Finisher Amplification" affixes join the amp stat; Combo Damage Enhancement — including Recuperation's — has its Enhancement affixes **sum into the `additional.combo_enhancement` bucket**, which is then its own [additional](#additional) multiplier. See the Combo rows in the table.)*
- **Fervor is not an additional-damage layer *by itself***: its native base effect is +2% Attack & Spell Crit Rating per Fervor Rating (max 100), which belongs in crit-rating math. It becomes damage only when gear GRANTS it a damage base effect — see the Ghost Slaughter row.
- **Strength is "additional", not "increased"**: tlidb: "every point of Strength **additionally** increases the damage of skills that have Strength as a main stat by 0.5%". One summed multiplier, not part of the increased pool. `increased.strength_derived` should be treated as a single additional layer (or accept the small error).
- **Numb is lightning-only** (+5% additional *Lightning* damage taken per stack) → no-op for a 100% cold build.
- **Ice Bond is a self-buff skill** (Euphoria: additional Cold Damage vs Frostbitten enemies), not an enemy debuff. Bucket placement is mathematically equivalent, but it is not in this build.
- Loadout 0 has **no warcry** (Bull's Rage + Fixate empower loop instead) and does **not equip Heart of Animitta** (necklace is Vortex Heart; Animitta appears only in loadouts 1–6).
- Loadout 0 is **dual-wield** (offhand "Unforgotten Long Blade"), not sword+shield.

## Answers to the critical questions

1. **Conversion double-dipping** — Yes. Converted damage benefits from modifiers of the original AND resulting type; dev-confirmed as intended ("conversion chains … allows both effects to take effect"). Order: base (incl. flat adds) → conversion (low→high priority: Physical→Lightning→Cold→Fire→Erosion, hits only) → all % modifiers with any matching tag apply. Physical, Cold, Elemental, Attack, Melee tags all hit the same increased pool for this build.
2. **Increased vs additional = PoE increased vs more** — Yes. `Damage = Base × (1 + Σ non-additional %) × Π(1 + each additional %)`. Each "additional" source multiplies separately (in-game text even annotates per-stack multiplication with "(multiplies)").
3. **Crit** — `chance = Final Crit Rating / 100`; `Final Rating = Σ base rating × (1 + Σ increased rating%) × Π(1 + additional rating%)` — user-provided official text 2026-07-15. All units have 150% base Critical Strike Damage; a crit deals `original damage × Critical Strike Damage %`. Crit-damage % affixes are assumed one additive pool over the 150% (still inferred — the official text doesn't state pool additivity).
4. **Ailments** — Frostbite: +1% additional Cold Damage taken per Frostbite Rating (base max 120); Frostbite Effect scales the debuff, Max Frostbite Rating raises the ceiling. Numb: +5% additional *Lightning* taken/stack → no-op here. Ice Bond: self Euphoria buff (+28% additional Cold vs Frostbitten at L20; patch 2026-07 nerf from 33%), not an enemy debuff. Paralysis: flat +15% additional damage taken, 1 stack. Cold Infiltration: −10% cold res AND +13% additional Cold taken.
5. **Combo Finisher Amplification** — additive with the skill's inherent +30% into a single amp stat; the finisher then gets ONE multiplier `(1 + consumed points × total amp)`. Animitta's +45–80% is huge because it's multiplied by points consumed.
6. **Armor / armor pen** — a second mitigation layer (user-corrected 2026-07-15, supersedes the earlier "cold res only" reading): monsters carry ~30% elemental resistance AND ~30% elemental reduction from armor. Armor DMG Mitigation Penetration subtracts from the armor layer exactly as elemental pen does from res, and both can push their layer negative (do not clamp at 0).

## Assumptions & open interpretations <a id="assumptions"></a>

What could move the numbers, migrated from the retired RANKINGS.md and kept current here:

- **Monster mitigation is two ~30% layers** (elemental res + armor elemental reduction — user-reported defaults). Both `enemy.cold_res_pct` and `enemy.armor_reduction_pct` are editable in `data/snapshot.json`; tougher bosses raise both, raising both penetrations' value.
- **Clone shotgun falloff read as a flat 70%** for every hit on the same target after the first. If it compounds per hit (0.7^n), the 9-clone finisher is heavily overstated — the single biggest open interpretation in the model.
- **Full uptimes assumed**: Fervor at the fixed 130 rating (Unmatched Valor prism — with fixed rating the Ronin gain/consume lines are treated as no-ops), Pure Heart at 5 stacks (requires dashing with Thunder Spike), Berserk/Rage Infusion stacks, boss Freeze for Cold Infiltration, Willpower's 6 still-stacks (0.5s grace after moving), blessing stacks (4 base +1 Focus).
- **Sustained Life deficit assumed 60%** (`LIFE_LOST_PCT` in build_parser) for "per 1% of Life lost" talents: Ghost Slaughter's 12%/s drain vs sustain sources, held above the 35% Low Life line (user 2026-07-16). The real equilibrium depends on regen — edit the constant to retune.
- **Low-life execute lines averaged over HP share** (harmonic mean, 35% threshold) — assumes even damage pacing across the boss's HP bar.
- **The build sits permanently at Low Life** (Seal Conversion reserves life below the 35% line, user 2026-07-17). In the catalog this inverts the self-low-life applicability filter (`NOT_THIS_BUILD` in catalog.ts): "when not at Low Life" gains are dead (Survival Will's +30% → `—`), "at Low Life" gains stay valid (No Loose Ends still `—`, but only because it zeroes ES).
